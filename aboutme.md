I am a biomedical scientist moving into health data science. My interest sits where clinical and commercial data turn into decisions: cohort studies on real electronic health records, statistical modelling, and reporting that non technical stakeholders actually use.

You can see the code behind my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/johannaalbers), or read the short version on the [projects page](/projects).

### Education

- MSc Biomedicine, University of Münster, Germany (2022 to 2025)
- BSc Biology, University of Münster, Germany (2018 to 2022)

### Experience

- Marketing Manager, B&F Oberwerth (current)

### What I work with

- **Languages:** R, Python, SQL
- **Statistics:** multivariable regression, survival analysis, missing data and multiple imputation, model validation
- **Machine learning:** scikit-learn, tree based models, SHAP
- **Health data:** MIMIC-III (credentialed PhysioNet access), cohort definition, EHR text mining, research data governance under a Data Use Agreement
- **Reporting:** Power BI, Quarto, R Markdown
